# GariSeba v2.8: payments, appointments and customer history

## Upgrade

Extract v2.8 into a separate folder. Copy your existing `.env` from v2.7 into the new `gariseba-mern` folder. Use the same MongoDB server and database so requests, staff accounts and private tracking keys carry over. Keep your current port, secrets and provider keys.

From that new folder:

```bash
npm ci
npm run build
npm start
```

Use the address printed by the server. A working port 3030 configuration stays on port 3030. Do not reseed the existing database to upgrade. These features require no new API keys, payment gateway or external calendar account. The app creates its new collections and indexes when it connects. Its existing backup command includes the new customer and appointment collections while continuing to exclude browser sessions.

## 1. Bill payment status

1. Complete a service request and issue its PDF bill as in v2.7.
2. In the request's **Bill payments** panel, record money the team has already received. Enter the amount in BDT, choose a method and optionally enter a transaction reference and internal note.
3. The app calculates the paid amount, remaining balance and status. Viewers can read these records but cannot add payments.
4. If money has actually been refunded, choose **Refund given** and record that amount. Earlier entries remain visible with their actor and timestamp.
5. The customer sees the current summary on private tracking, in signed-in service history and in a newly downloaded bill.

Example for a BDT 1,000.10 bill:

| Recorded entry | Net paid | Balance | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| No entries | 0.00 | 1,000.10 | Unpaid |
| Payment 400.05 | 400.05 | 600.05 | Partially paid |
| Payment 600.05 | 1,000.10 | 0.00 | Paid |
| Refund 100.10 | 900.00 | 100.10 | Partially paid |

Amounts are stored as integer poisha. A payment cannot exceed the current balance; a refund cannot exceed the net paid amount. Zero or negative entries are rejected. A zero-total bill is Paid with a zero balance. Each bill supports up to 200 payment/refund entries.

Entries cannot be edited or removed through this form. Corrections use appropriate additional payment/refund entries. A bill cannot be revised below the amount already recorded as paid until an appropriate refund is recorded. Saving a new entry requires the current request version. An operation ID prevents duplicate entries if the browser retries after a lost response. Different concurrent edits cannot both save against the same version.

This is a record of payments handled outside the app. Selecting bKash, Nagad or bank does not connect to that provider, charge the customer or verify a transaction ID. Staff enter these records after checking the actual payment. Existing v2.7 bills have no payment ledger and initially show Unpaid in the app; enter any previously collected payments explicitly. Existing bill notes are not converted into payment records.

## 2. Appointment booking

### Staff availability

An owner or dispatcher opens **Appointments**, chooses a date and hour in Dhaka time, and selects **Publish one-hour slot**. Each published slot accepts one service request. Slots start on a whole hour and last exactly one hour, so the shared schedule has no overlapping slots. Duplicate hours are rejected.

Slots must start at least 30 minutes ahead and can be published up to 90 days ahead. Staff explicitly choose availability; no opening hours or technician capacity are assumed. This release provides one shared service schedule. It does not automatically assign a technician or calculate travel time.

Unbooked future slots can be closed or reopened. Viewers can inspect the schedule but cannot publish, close or cancel slots. A booking shows its service reference and customer name only to authorized staff, with a link to the service request. Public availability exposes only open, unbooked times.

### Customer booking

- For a guest request, open `/appointments`, enter the reference and private tracking key, and choose **Find my service request**. You can also open appointments from the tracking result.
- For a signed-in request, open **My services**, choose **View service**, then **Service appointments**. Access is checked against the account on the server.
- Choose a date, then **Book appointment** on an available time. The request must be open: Received, Confirmed or In progress.
- Each request can hold one reservation at a time. Use **Cancel appointment** before choosing a different slot. Cancelling releases that time for another customer. A passed reservation is read-only for customers; staff can cancel it if an open service needs rescheduling.

There is no availability until staff publish slots. A slot can become unavailable while someone is looking at it; refresh and choose another time if the reservation conflicts. Database uniqueness and atomic updates prevent two requests from reserving the same slot and prevent one request from holding two slots.

Staff can cancel bookings from their schedule. A cancelled slot remains open unless staff close it. Booking or cancelling does not send additional paid SMS/email; the existing status-change notifications retain their earlier behavior.

## 3. Customer login and service history

Open **My services** at `/account` and create a customer account. Usernames use 3-40 English letters/digits with optional dots, underscores or hyphens and start with a letter or digit. They are case-insensitive. Your display name can use Bangla or English. Passwords need at least 15 characters and at most 72 UTF-8 bytes; Bangla characters use more bytes.

Customer accounts are separate from staff accounts. Registering the same username as an existing staff member creates a separate customer identity and grants no staff permissions. New customer credentials do not alter existing staff passwords.

### Save the recovery code

Registration shows a private recovery code once. Save it in a safe place. The app stores only its hash. **Recover account** needs the username, recovery code and a new password, and replaces the code with a new one. It requires no email or SMS integration. Without both a usable password and saved recovery code, there is no automatic recovery in this release.

Signed-in customers can also use **Change password** with their current password. Changing or recovering the password replaces the recovery code and invalidates earlier customer sessions. Repeated incorrect sign-in attempts temporarily lock the account. Customer login regenerates the browser session, and server checks protect every history and bill read.

Customer login/logout preserves an existing staff login in the same browser. The inherited staff sign-in/logout flow can end a customer session in that browser. These remain separate account roles; use separate browser profiles when testing both roles at the same time.

### Add and view services

- Requests submitted while signed in are associated with that account automatically.
- Older guest requests need their reference and private tracking key under **Add an earlier request**. Phone numbers alone cannot search or claim private history. An already linked request cannot move to a different account through this form.
- History is paginated at 20 services per page. Open a service to see its status history, issued bill, recorded payment balance, saved feedback and appointment.
- **Refresh history** reads the latest data. Bill downloads are authorized by the signed-in account; the app does not need to display or reconstruct a secret tracking key.
- Guests retain the existing request and private tracking flow. **Continue as a guest** clears a customer login before opening the request form.

## Permissions

| Role | Payments | Appointment schedule | Private customer history |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Owner / dispatcher | Read and record payments/refunds | Read, publish, close/reopen and cancel | No customer login is granted by the staff role |
| Staff viewer | Read only | Read only | No customer login is granted by the staff role |
| Technician | Existing assigned-work permissions; no billing access | No global schedule access | No customer login is granted by the technician role |
| Signed-in customer | Read own bill summary and PDF | Book/cancel for own open requests | Own requests and correctly linked legacy requests |
| Guest with reference/key | Read that request's bill summary/PDF | Book/cancel for that request | No account history listing |

## Deletion and compatibility

Deleting an active service request removes its local invoice, payment entries, feedback and photos, and releases its appointment reservation. It does not delete a separate customer login. Previously downloaded bills and backups retain their own copies. Appointment handlers recheck a reservation so a deletion race cannot recreate the deleted request or leave a new orphan reservation.

The existing AI assistant, guest form, staff filters, technician accounts and assignments, arrival estimates, email/SMS integrations, reports and CSV remain in place. Configuration and provider credentials are not changed by the upgrade. Payment records and booking changes do not create duplicate status-change notifications.

## Verify in your environment

1. Create a customer account and save its recovery code.
2. Submit a new request while signed in. Confirm it appears in history. Link an older guest request using its original reference/key.
3. As staff, publish a future slot. Book it from the customer history, cancel it, and check that it becomes available again.
4. Complete the service, issue a bill and record a partial payment, the remaining payment and a refund. Check the paid amount and balance in tracking and in the downloaded PDF.
5. Sign in as a viewer and confirm that billing and schedule changes are unavailable. Test two customer accounts to ensure neither can read the other's history or PDF.
6. Change a customer password or use recovery; confirm that an earlier session can no longer load history. Existing staff logins should still work.
